## Risk Assessment

Risk is based on **likelihood** and **consequence**:

$$
\text{Risk} = \text{Likelihood} \times \text{Consequence}
$$

Each is scored from **1–5**, giving a total risk score from **1–25**.

| Factor | Description |
|---|---|
| **Likelihood (1–5)** | How likely is the failure to occur? |
| **Consequence (1–5)** | How serious is the impact if it occurs? |

The same criteria will be used for every design option so they can be compared consistently. The risk score is a relative score for comparing design choices, not an actual probability of failure.


# Mission Requirements

## Top-Level Requirements

### Cost Requirement
- The mission shall be as inexpensive and practical as possible.
- The mission shall provide a detailed breakdown of estimated costs.

### Timeline Requirement
- The mission shall rendezvous with the target before it reaches an altitude of 300 km.

### Technology Readiness Requirement
- The mission shall implement technologies with a Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of 6 or higher.
- The mission shall incorporate at least one TRL 4 or 5 technology for flight demonstration.
- The mission shall provide proper citations for all technologies with a TRL below 6.

### Mission Objective Baseline
- The mission shall raise the orbit of the Swift Observatory from 300 km to 600 km without damaging any equipment below nominal operational levels.

# Mission Parameters

- **Initial Orbit:** 300 km
- **Target Orbit:** 600 km
- **Maximum Rendezvous Altitude:** 300 km
- **Minimum Technology Readiness Level:** TRL 6
- **Flight Demonstration Technology:** At least one TRL 4 or 5 technology
